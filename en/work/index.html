The work

Several doors into the same attention.

Bronze Art photographs weddings, sacraments, families and portraits without reducing them to empty categories. Each area belongs to the same question: what deserves to remain?

Weddings

A promise, two families, a church, a house, a table. Photographed without turning the day into performance.

Sacraments

Baptism, First Communion and Confirmation as events of the Church lived within the concrete history of a family.

View sacraments

Families in Fátima

Local and pilgrim families photographed in a place where faith, silence, stone and generations meet.

View families

Classical portraits

Portraits of presence, light and character. No excessive artifice, no haste, no disposable appearance.

View portraits
