Catholic Sacraments

A visible beginning, witnessed by family and the Church.

A Baptism, a First Communion or a Confirmation belongs to the life of the Church, and also to the memory of a particular family.

The work respects the liturgy, the silence and the presence of those gathered: parents, godparents, grandparents and children.
