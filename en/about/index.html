About

I photograph from within a life that is also my own.

I live near Fátima with my wife and our children. It is there that I learned the weight of a full table, a blessing, a child growing up and a photograph that remains.

Theology and family life taught me to look at promise, sacrament, parents and grandparents. Not as scenery, but as the concrete history a photograph should know how to keep.
