About

The way I photograph comes from the way I see family.

I live near Fátima with my wife and our children. Family, faith and time are not abstract subjects to me. They are where life becomes visible.

My formation in Theology and family life have given me a particular attention to what remains: promise, sacrament, parents and grandparents, childhood before it changes, and the quiet beauty of a house or a church.
