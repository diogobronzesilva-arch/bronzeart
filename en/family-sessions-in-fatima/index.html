Families in Fátima

A family portrait with place, silence and continuity.

Some families come to Fátima as pilgrims. Others live nearby and return often. In both cases, the place gives the photograph a different weight.

The Sanctuary, the stone, the light and the paths are not decoration. They belong to the family story of that day.
