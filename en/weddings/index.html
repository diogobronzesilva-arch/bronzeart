Weddings

A promise, two families, a place.

A wedding gathers more than a day. It gathers parents, grandparents, friends, a church, a house, a table and the quiet signs of a life beginning.

The work follows the day with attention, without turning the celebration into performance. The final gallery gathers the photographs that belong to the story, individually selected and edited.
