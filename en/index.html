Fátima, Portugal

Photography for family, faith and the things time would otherwise carry away.

Bronze Art is a photographic work rooted in Fátima, dedicated to weddings, Catholic sacraments, family stories and classical portraiture. The work is documentary in attention, classical in spirit and quietly respectful of what each family entrusts to the camera.
